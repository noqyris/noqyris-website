Foldwing — Privacy Policy

Last updated 27 July 2026

Foldwing is a puzzle game published by Djordje Subotic. This policy explains what the app collects, what it does not, and who else is involved.

What Foldwing itself collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no login and no server of its own. Your progress, your settings and the drawings you save are stored only on your device, and are deleted when you delete the app. Nothing you draw is uploaded anywhere. If you tap Share, the image is handed to your device's own share sheet and you choose where it goes — Foldwing does not send it anywhere itself.

Advertising

Foldwing shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect and process:

  • advertising identifiers, including Apple's IDFA where you have allowed it
  • coarse device and app information, such as device model and OS version
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown and whether they were tapped
  • approximate location derived from your IP address

This data is collected by Google under Google's own privacy terms. On iOS, Foldwing asks for tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads are still shown but are requested as non-personalised, and no advertising identifier is used to build a profile of you.

Purchases

Foldwing offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; the app never sees your payment details. Once purchased, no ad requests are made at all.

Children

Foldwing is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off personalised ads at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on iOS.
  • Buy Remove Ads to stop ad requests entirely.
  • Delete the app to erase everything it has stored on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
