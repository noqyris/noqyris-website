Shear Line: Lockpick Puzzle — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026. Applies to Shear Line version 1.0 and later.

Overview

Shear Line is a lockpicking puzzle game. There are no accounts and no sign-in, and we, the developer, run no servers: nothing you do in the game is sent to us. The game does show ads, and the ad service it uses collects some data from your device. This policy explains what is collected, why, and how you control it.

Advertising

Unless you own Remove Ads, the game can show two kinds of ads: short videos you choose to watch for a stated reward (for example, to see every gate of a lock for a few seconds, to put a jammed pin back, to double the loot from a lock, or to keep your Night Shift streak), and an occasional full-screen ad when you leave the result of a lock you opened. A full-screen ad never interrupts a lock you are picking and never follows a failed attempt; a reward video plays only when you tap to watch it. There is no banner.

These ads are provided by Unity LevelPlay, an ad mediation service run by Unity Technologies, with ads from the Unity Ads network. The ad software is part of the app, but it sends data directly to Unity, not to us, and Unity handles that data under its own privacy policy.

What Unity and its ad partners may collect

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks. If you don't, iOS gives the app a blank identifier instead.
  • The identifier for vendor (IDFV), an identifier iOS gives each developer's apps on your device.
  • Your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived.
  • Device and system information, such as device model, operating system version, language and connection type.
  • Ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown, whether a video was watched to the end, and taps on ads.
  • Diagnostic data, such as performance measurements and technical error information about how ads load and display.

What it is used for

  • Choosing and showing ads, and limiting how often the same ad appears.
  • Measuring how ads perform and which ads lead to an app install.
  • Personalising ads, only with your consent and, for the advertising identifier, your tracking permission.
  • Detecting fraud and invalid ad activity.
  • Keeping the ad software working: finding and fixing problems with how ads load, display and perform.

Your choices

The ad software does not start while you play your first lock: at first there are no ads and no tracking or ad-consent prompts. After that, before any ad is loaded, the game first asks for your permission:

  • Tracking (iOS App Tracking Transparency). If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier is not shared, so ads cannot be personalised with it. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad consent. A Shear Line prompt asks whether our ad partners may use data from your device, such as its advertising identifier, to show and measure ads. If you tap Accept, the ad software starts. If you tap Decline, the ad software is not started at all, so it collects nothing and no ads are shown, including the optional reward videos. The whole game still plays.

If you accept the ad consent but decline tracking, ads are still shown, but your advertising identifier is not used to personalise them.

To change your ad consent later, open Settings → Privacy choices in the game. It shows the same prompt again. If you withdraw consent after ads have started, the game stops showing and loading ads and tells the ad service not to sell or share your personal information. Privacy choices is not shown when the ad software is not in use.

If you own Remove Ads, the ad software is not started once the game knows about your purchase on this device, and the optional reward perks are yours without watching anything. After you reinstall the game or move to a new device, tap Restore in the store or in Settings so the game knows again.

Many Unity ads also show a privacy (shield) icon that you can tap to opt out of certain personalised ads. For more on how Unity uses this data and the choices you have, read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. To make a privacy request about ad data, contact Unity as described in its policy: we don't hold that data.

In-app purchases

All purchases are optional: Remove Ads, the Pro Pick Set, the Starter Kit and loot packs. Purchases are processed by Apple. The app never sees your payment details, name or Apple ID, and we receive no personal information about you from a purchase. What you own is remembered on your device, and Restore (in the store and in Settings) asks Apple to re-apply your one-time purchases, for example after you reinstall. Loot you buy is stored on your device and never expires, but like the rest of your progress it is lost if you delete the app.

Reminders (local notifications)

Shear Line can send at most one reminder a day, for example when tonight's Night Shift lock is ready or your streak is about to end. The game only asks iOS for permission after you tap to turn reminders on, and nothing is sent unless you allow notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device by iOS; nothing is sent to us or to a push server. To pick a good time, the game remembers on your device the hours at which you usually play. You can turn reminders off at any time in the game's Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Shear Line.

Data stored on your device

The game saves the following only on your device, in the app's own storage:

  • Your progress: locks opened, stars and best times, jobs and answers, loot, cosmetics, the Night Shift streak, daily missions, the Vault Run and play statistics.
  • Your settings: sound, haptics, visual cues and reminders, and the hours you usually play (used only to time reminders).
  • Which purchases you own, and the purchase transactions already credited, so loot is never granted twice.
  • Counters used to space out ads, such as when the last full-screen ad was shown and how many sessions you have played.
  • Your ad consent choice, which the ad software stores so you aren't asked again every launch.

None of it is sent to us, and all of it is removed if you delete the app.

Network

The game itself plays fully offline. It uses the network only to load ads (after you have given consent) and for purchases and restores through Apple.

Children's Privacy

Shear Line is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and it tells the ad software that it is not a child-directed app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's information has been collected through the ads in this game, contact us at the address below.
